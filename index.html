About Musala Safety Consultants

Every year, fire causes deaths and damages to property. Musala Safety Consultants exists to change that. We deal in all types of fire fighting and fire prevention systems as well as life safety equipment, and we are specialized in designing, supply, installation, testing, commissioning, servicing and maintenance of various fire protection and alarm systems. Our highly qualified and well-experienced team of engineers design, install and commission systems to meet NFPA and other international and local authority requirements — quality first, always.
